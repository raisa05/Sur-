Member 1: The Shopper POV (Focus on Discovery)

1. Map Explorer

Large map with pins. Bottom drawer peeks up showing a selected store's surplus.

2. Swipe to Save

Tinder-style interface. Large photo of food. Swipe right to claim, left to skip.

3. Countdown List

Vertical feed focusing on urgency. Large countdown timers (e.g. "Expires 2h").

4. Lock Screen Alerts

Push notifications dominating the experience. "Your favorite bakery dropped 5 bags!"

5. Impact Dashboard

Gamified profile. Charts showing CO2 saved, money saved, and badges earned.

6. AR Discovery

Looking through the camera, floating digital tags hover over real store fronts.

7. Recipe Matcher

Input "I have pasta", app shows nearby surplus tomatoes and basil.

8. Mystery Box Sub

Toggle switch to subscribe to a weekly random surplus bag based on dietary prefs.

Member 2: The Retailer POV (Focus on Inventory)

1. Quick Snap Upload

Camera interface is the home screen. Snap a pic, AI tags it, instantly listed.

2. 1-Click Templates

Buttons for common items: "End of Day Pastries", "Mixed Veggie Bag".

3. Voice Inventory

Microphone button dominates. Retailer speaks: "List 5 loaves of bread."

4. POS Auto-Sync

Dashboard showing link to Cash Register. Unsold stock auto-lists at 5 PM.

5. Drop Schedule

Calendar view. Drag and drop "Surplus bags" onto specific days/times.

6. Pickup QR Scanner

Scanner mode to verify a customer picking up their reserved bag.

7. Store Leaderboard

Friendly neighborhood competition showing which store saved the most food.

8. Auto-Pricing Slider

Slider sets rule: "Drop price by 10% every hour until closed."

Member 3: Volunteer POV (Focus on Logistics)

1. Volunteer Route Map

Connects 3 stores to 1 food bank, showing optimal driving route.

2. Surplus Heatmap

City map showing red zones (high surplus) vs blue zones (high demand).

3. Community Request Board

Forum style UI where residents can "request" items, driving store supply.

4. Volunteer Matcher

Swipe-style UI to match available drivers with pending bulk surplus runs.

5. Impact PDF Generator

One-click export showing local government the waste reduced.

6. Neighborhood Goal

Giant progress bar filling up: "1,000 meals saved together!"

7. Triple Chat

Group chat UI: Store owner, Driver, and Food Bank coordinating pickup.

8. Megaphone Alert

Big red button to push an emergency alert for highly perishable bulk drops.
